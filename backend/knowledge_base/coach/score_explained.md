# Driving Coach Guide - How the DriveSense Score Works

## The seven events
DriveSense watches for seven driving events: Over Speed Limit (OSL), Accelerating Before Turn (ABT), Braking After Turn (BAT), Frequent Acceleration and Braking (FAB), Harsh Acceleration (HA), Harsh Braking (HB) and Harsh Turn (HT). They are detected from the vehicle's speed and heading once per second: acceleration is the change of speed per second, and sideways force is speed multiplied by how fast the heading is changing.

## The score formula
Every trip starts at 100. Each event costs points according to its weight - Harsh Braking 5, Harsh Turn 4, Harsh Acceleration 3, Over Speed Limit 3, Frequent Acceleration and Braking 3, Accelerating Before Turn 2, Braking After Turn 2. The points are scaled per 10 km driven (with a minimum distance of 5 km), so a long trip with one event scores higher than a short trip with the same event. The score cannot go below 0.

## Score bands
0 to 74 is Okay, 75 to 84 is Fair, 85 to 99 is Good and 100 is Excellent. The weekly score is the average of trip scores weighted by distance, so longer trips count more.

## Leaderboard
Your rank compares your 7-day weekly score with all other drivers, and with drivers of the same vehicle model. Improving one habit - usually harsh braking or speeding - is the fastest way to climb.
